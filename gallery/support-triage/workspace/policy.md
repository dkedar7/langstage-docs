# Northwind Outfitters: customer care policy

Applies to orders delivered in the last 30 days.

## Refunds

- **Defective or damaged on arrival:** refund the item price in full. The customer
  keeps or recycles the item; no return needed for items under $250.
- **Late delivery on a paid express order:** refund the express shipping fee.
- **Changed mind:** no refund. Offer a free exchange or store credit instead.
- Never refund more than the order total.
- **Any refund over $100 needs a care manager's approval.** `issue_refund` asks for it
  automatically; don't promise the money to the customer until it is approved.

## Exchanges

- Free size or color exchange within 30 days of delivery, if the item is unworn.
- Send the customer the prepaid exchange link: https://northwind.example/exchange

## Order status

- Give the carrier, the tracking number and the expected delivery date from the order.

## Tone

- Warm, short and specific. Use the customer's first name and the order number.
- Say what we did, and what happens next. Sign off as "Northwind Customer Care".
