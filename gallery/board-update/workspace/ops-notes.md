# Ops notes for the Q3 board update

From Dana (operations), Oct 8.

- Returns jumped in August and September. Most of the jump is one product: the
  Ridgeline 2P tent. 212 of them came back with cracked poles, all from the same
  supplier batch (Lot 7, shipped in July).
- We refunded or replaced every one. Support's policy of a full refund for a defective
  item held up; average time to resolve was 1.8 days.
- Without the Ridgeline returns, the Q3 return rate would be about 4.8%, in line with Q2.
- The tent poles come from a single supplier. A second supplier has passed our drop and
  cold tests and can start in November.
- Fix we want approved: move poles to the new supplier ($85,000 in tooling and the first
  order) and extend the Ridgeline warranty to two years ($35,000 reserve). $120,000 total.
- NPS fell from 46 to 41. Comments that mention the tent account for most of the drop.
